03 Permits

The city has no record of the finished basement or the third garage stall

Battle Creek's assessing record for 130 Turnberry shows 0 sq ft of finished basement and a 721 sq ft, 2-car garage. The same records show 191 Turnberry's 1,425 sq ft finished basement and 1,011 sq ft, 3-car garage, so the city does track both when work is permitted.

Battle Creek requires a building permit to finish a basement, with egress windows, hardwired smoke detectors and eight inspections, and a garage addition always needs one. If the work was permitted, the assessor's record would normally have been updated. If it was not, three things follow:

  • Appraisal. The appraiser starts from the city's record. Unpermitted finished space is usually discounted or left out, which can leave a buyer short at closing.
  • Disclosure. Michigan's seller disclosure asks about additions and structural changes and whether permits were pulled. We answer it accurately.
  • Buyer confidence. A buyer's inspector will ask. Permits on file end the conversation; no permits start one.

What to do: check your records, and call the Inspections Division at 269-966-3382 with the address. If no permits were pulled, you can apply as the homeowner and have the work inspected now. That takes a few weeks and some cost, and it turns the basement and garage into assets an appraiser can count. The price below assumes the work checks out; if it stays unpermitted, I would list at $459,900 and disclose it.
