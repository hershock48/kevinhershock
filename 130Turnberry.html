09 Listing plan

A listing aimed at families shopping the Lakeview district

  • Professional photos and a strong lead photoTaken in daylight before the leaves are gone, with twilight exteriors and drone shots of the landscaped lot.
  • Floor plan and 3D tourSo buyers see the main-floor primary suite, the hearth room and the upstairs bonus room before they visit.
  • The lower level presented on purposeIf it is unfinished, we show the 9-foot ceilings and daylight windows as space ready to finish, with a contractor's estimate on hand for buyers.
  • Open house the first weekendTimed so buyers touring the other Fairway Downs listings see yours as the value in the neighborhood.
  • Targeted online adsTo buyers in Battle Creek, Marshall and Kalamazoo in this price range, plus a mailer to Fairway Downs and Minges Hills neighbors.
  • A weekly report to youShowings, feedback and online views, so any later price decision is based on what buyers are doing.

Getting ready, about 10 days

  1. Days 1 and 2Gather dates and receipts for the roof, furnace, air conditioner, water heater and any remodeling, plus a recent well water test if you have one.
  2. Days 2 to 5Touch up paint, replace burnt-out bulbs, service the furnace and clean the gutters.
  3. Days 3 to 6Clear counters and closets, set up the bonus room as a fifth bedroom or office, and clean and light the lower level.
  4. Days 5 to 7Mow, edge, fresh mulch and fall planters by the front door.
  5. Days 7 and 8Photos, floor plan and 3D tour.
  6. Days 9 and 10Go live on a Thursday, with an open house that weekend.
